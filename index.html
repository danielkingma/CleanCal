Product spec · Built from Breezeway + Turno

Every turnover, tracked, cleaned, and paid — in one calendar.

01

Market positioning

Existing tools force a choice: run your own cleaning team (Breezeway) or tap a marketplace of gig cleaners (Turno). CleanCal treats that as a false choice — most managers of 50+ properties need both, depending on the week.

  • Hybrid model — internal team management and an on-demand cleaner marketplace, side by side.
  • AI quality prediction — computer vision reads the photo evidence before a manager has to.
  • Dynamic pricing — cleaner pay flexes with urgency, occupancy, and season automatically.
  • Gamification — points, badges, and leaderboards to keep crews engaged.
  • Budget pricing — enterprise features at 40–60% below the cost of running Breezeway + Turno together.
  • Everything connected on day one — Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com, not "coming soon." No integration with Guesty or Hostaway, since they're direct competitors.
02

Core architecture

A conventional, boring-on-purpose stack — the differentiation lives in the product, not the plumbing.

  • Web — React 18 + TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn/ui.
  • Mobile — React Native + Expo, one codebase for iOS and Android.
  • Backend — Node.js (NestJS), GraphQL.
  • Data — PostgreSQL, Redis for real-time state, S3 for photo evidence.
  • AI — AWS Rekognition or TensorFlow.js for photo analysis, OpenAI for written quality summaries.
  • Payments — Stripe Connect for multi-currency payouts.
  • Hosting — AWS, or Vercel + Supabase to move faster pre-launch.
03

User roles & access

Seven roles, scoped so each person sees exactly what their job requires — nothing more.

Role 1

Property Owner / Host

  • Full control: properties, billing, integrations
  • Hires from marketplace or builds internal team
  • Sees all analytics and quality scores
Role 2

Property Manager

  • Operational oversight, no billing access
  • Assigns jobs, approves photo submissions
  • Views task completion dashboards
Role 3

Cleaning Team Lead

  • Assigns jobs within their own team
  • Sees team performance and rankings
  • No access to other properties or finances
Role 4

Individual Cleaner

  • Mobile-first: tasks, checklists, photos
  • Moves jobs through the 3-stage workflow
  • Tracks own earnings, points, badge progress
Role 5

Maintenance / Handyman

  • Receives issues flagged by cleaners
  • Updates repair status with photo proof
  • No visibility into cleaning schedules
Role 6

Guest Services / Front Desk

  • View-only: is the unit clean and ready?
  • No scheduling or assignment rights
Role 7

Accountant / Bookkeeper

  • View-only financial reports
  • Exports to QuickBooks / Xero
  • Cannot touch schedules or operations
04

Unified calendar

The surface everyone lives in. Bookings from every connected channel land here automatically, with cleaning blocks generated the moment a reservation confirms.

Views

  • Week — seven days, color-coded by cleaning status.
  • Month — full property grid: occupancy layered against cleaning state.
  • Year — seasonal load and planning at a glance.
  • Agenda — a flat list of what's next, sorted by urgency.

Auto-scheduling

  • Creates the cleaning task the moment a booking confirms via API.
  • Suggests windows from checkout/check-in times.
  • Flags same-day turnovers as priority automatically.
  • Accounts for a cleaner's existing load and travel time.
05

Cleaning task management

Every job moves through the same three stages, visible to owner, manager, and cleaner alike.

To Clean
Property details and instructions unlock; cleaner starts the timer on arrival.
In Progress
Photos, checklist, and notes are captured as the job happens.
Complete
Final photos submitted, AI quality check runs, payment releases.

Photo requirements

  • Mandatory before/after shots, room by room.
  • Inventory verification — linens, toiletries, coffee pods.
  • GPS and timestamp embedded automatically.
  • Computer vision flags likely misses before a human reviews.

Checklists & notes

  • Custom templates per property or property type.
  • Disposable-inventory counts baked into the flow.
  • Damage or maintenance issues route automatically to the right person.
  • Optional voice-to-text for hands-free notes.

07

Cleaner marketplace

A two-sided market sitting alongside internal team tools — use either, or both, per property.

For managers

  • Browse by location, availability, rating, and price.
  • Post urgent jobs at premium pay to pull fast responders.
  • Save preferred cleaners for recurring bookings.

For cleaners

  • Professional profile: photo, bio, certifications.
  • Accept or decline jobs instantly from the app.
  • Build reputation and climb toward higher-paying work.

Trust & safety

  • Background checks via Checkr.
  • Government ID + selfie identity verification.
  • Two-way ratings — cleaners rate properties too.
  • Admin-arbitrated dispute resolution.

09

AI differentiators

The three things Breezeway and Turno don't do — together, or at all.

Quality prediction

Computer vision scores every uploaded photo (0–100) for missed spots, clutter, and staging, then flags a re-clean before a guest ever sees it. The model improves as managers confirm or override its calls.

Dynamic pricing

Cleaner pay adjusts automatically for same-day urgency (+25–50%), property size, top-rated cleaner premiums, seasonal demand, and travel distance — inside an owner-set cap.

Gamification

  • Points for completions, five-star ratings, and perfect checklists.
  • Badges — "Speed Demon," "Perfectionist," "Photo Pro," and similar milestones.
  • Regional and global leaderboards, reset monthly.
  • Top performers get featured placement in the marketplace.

Mobile apps

iOS and Android from one React Native codebase, built around the cleaner's day in the field.

Must be on mobile

  • Week / Month / Year calendar with swipe navigation.
  • Task status updates: To Clean → In Progress → Complete.
  • In-app camera with GPS tagging, offline-capable.
  • Digital checklists and inventory counts.
  • Voice-to-text notes, push notifications, one-tap navigation to the property.

Fine on web only

  • Portfolio management and bulk operations.
  • Analytics dashboards and integration setup.
  • Billing, roles, and marketplace vetting.

Go-to-market

Beta — month 6

  • 10–20 managers with 50+ properties each, six months free for feedback.
  • Focus markets: Los Angeles, Miami, Austin, Denver, Vancouver.

Public launch — months 8–9

  • US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand first; EU follows 3–6 months later pending GDPR review.
  • Trade shows, rental-association partnerships, referral program (one month free per referral).
